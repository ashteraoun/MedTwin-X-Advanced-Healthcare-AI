# Limitations

- The current product is a Laravel synthetic-cohort explorer with a deterministic descriptive-analysis service, not a multimodal foundation model or clinical digital-twin prediction system.
- Demo signal names and arbitrary units are fabricated software fixtures without clinical semantics.
- No general patient ingestion, document processing, medical NLP, image processing, learned embeddings, external vector/graph database, medical RAG corpus, trained model inference, or formal privacy-preserving federated training is implemented. Agent stages are deterministic structured scaffolding; evidence/risk stages abstain.
- The research tables provide storage structure, not complete authorization policies, lifecycle workflows, validation, encryption, or retention controls.
- Dataset status values do not replace license review, de-identification, or research approval.
- One software experiment measured static and temporal forecasting baselines on eight deterministic synthetic series. It is not representative; there are no performance, fairness, calibration, or clinical-validity claims.
- Synthetic cohort records are read-only through the dashboard. Twin snapshots, experiment metrics, and agent-stage outputs can be persisted for generated fixtures; these public demo workflows are throttled but are not a production authorization design for non-demo data.

Do not use this prototype for patient care or infer patient outcomes from its synthetic demo.
