The tiny fixture, deterministic generator, lack of uncertainty intervals, and absence of external validation prevent generalization claims. Federated sums are not a formal privacy mechanism. No other requested comparison has run and no clinical outcome is predicted.

Before federated experiments, specify client partitioning, aggregation method, threat model, communication payloads, and whether differential privacy is used. Federated training alone is not a privacy guarantee. Test that only model updates/approved aggregates leave each simulated silo and use synthetic data only.
